Privacy & credits

JustTrakt uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt. This independent project is not affiliated with JustWatch.

Your data stays in your browser profile

JustTrakt stores OAuth tokens, username, watch history, playback progress, watchlist snapshots, ratings and preferences in local extension storage. It has no developer backend, analytics, ads or telemetry.

What goes to Trakt

The extension sends HTTPS authentication, sync and title lookup requests to Trakt. Titles or external IDs read from JustWatch are used for matching. Clicking Add writes to your Trakt watchlist. Trakt tokens are never sent to JustWatch.

Your controls

Disconnect removes local account data and caches; preferences remain. It does not remove items from your Trakt account or revoke authorization there. Revoke access in Trakt settings if desired. Uninstall to clear remaining settings.

Ratings are cached for 24 hours (up to 1,500 titles), watchlist snapshots for five minutes. Expired entries can remain until replaced or cleared. Local browser storage is not an encrypted vault.

Read the full privacy policy and third-party notices included with this extension. For support, use the Issues tab in the repository you downloaded it from. Never post tokens or storage exports.
