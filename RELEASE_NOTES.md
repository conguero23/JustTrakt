# JustTrakt 1.7.3 (Beta)

Refines JustWatchIt cards:

- Full-height artwork through native picture wrappers.
- Compact gradient footer with ratings kept inside card edges.
- Clipped native circular controls moved inside the poster.
- Existing actions, rating icons and native handlers preserved.
- Original sizing and positioning restored when switching off.

Download **justtrakt-1.7.3.zip**, replace the extension files,
Reload in chrome://extensions, then refresh Trakt tabs.
Your login and settings remain unchanged.

Validated with DOM regression tests and local browser measurements for artwork,
footer, watch controls and hover-action bounds. Live Trakt layouts may vary.
